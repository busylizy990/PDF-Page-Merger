# PDF Page Merger — Privacy Notice

**Version 1.0 — 6 October 2026**

This notice explains what personal data we hold about you when you buy or use
PDF Page Merger, why we hold it, and what you can do about it. It is the notice
referred to in clause 8.3 of our [Licence Terms](LICENCE-TERMS.md), and is
available on request.

**Not legal advice.** This notice is drafted to a commercial standard but has not
been reviewed by a solicitor. Have it read alongside the Licence Terms before the
first paid sale.

---

## The short version

- **The Software holds nothing.** PDF Page Merger runs entirely on your own
  machine. It does not upload your documents, does not read their text, has no
  telemetry and needs no internet connection. We never see what you merge.
- **We hold only what we need to sell and support it:** your name, your work
  email address, your billing details, and any correspondence you send us.
- **We are not your data processor**, because your documents never reach us. You
  do not need a data processing agreement with us for the licence itself.
- You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold, and you can complain to the
  Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how we handle it.

## 2. The important point about the Software itself

PDF Page Merger operates only on the computer it is installed on. It copies page
objects between files locally. It does not transmit the contents of your
documents, does not extract or record their text, contains no analytics or usage
reporting, and functions with no internet connection at all.

Where the Software converts a Word document or a spreadsheet, that conversion is
performed by the copy of Microsoft Office or LibreOffice already installed on your
own machine. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

**It follows that we receive no personal data, and no confidential or privileged
information, through your use of the Software.** We cannot access your files. For
the documents you process we are neither a controller nor a processor, and no
data processing agreement is required between us for the licence.

Your own obligations in respect of those documents — your duty of confidentiality
to your clients, and your own responsibilities as a controller — are unaffected by
this notice.

## 4. A caution about support attachments

If you send us a document to help us reproduce a problem, that document comes to
us, and it may contain personal data or privileged material belonging to your
clients.

**Please do not send us a real client document if a redacted or invented one would
show the same problem.** Where you do send one, we will use it only to
investigate the problem you reported, we will not keep it after the problem is
closed, and we will delete it on request at any time. If you would prefer to
describe the problem rather than send the file, we will work with that.

## 8. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to:

- **be told** what we hold and why — this notice;
- **see** the personal data we hold about you;
- **have it corrected** if it is wrong or incomplete;
- **have it erased**, where we have no continuing lawful reason to keep it. Note
  that we cannot erase an invoice we are obliged by tax law to retain;
- **restrict** how we use it while a dispute about it is resolved;
- **receive it in a portable form**, for the data you gave us that we hold on the
  basis of our contract with you;
- **object** to use based on our legitimate interests; and
- **withdraw consent**, where we relied on your consent — for marketing, for
  example — without affecting anything done before you withdrew it.

We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling, so no rights arise
under Article 22.
