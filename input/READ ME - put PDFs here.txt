Put the PDFs, images, Word documents and spreadsheets you want merged here.

Images (jpg, png, gif, bmp, tif, tiff, webp) become pages just like PDF pages,
and can be mixed in freely. A multi-page TIFF becomes several pages.

Word documents (docx, doc, docm, rtf, odt) and spreadsheets (xlsx, xlsm, xls,
csv, ods) are converted to PDF automatically using Microsoft Office, or
LibreOffice if Office is unavailable, then merged in like anything else. The
converted copies are temporary and are deleted once the merge finishes.

Image pages are automatically given the same page size as the PDF pages they
are merged with, so the finished document is one consistent size throughout.

They are merged in natural name order, so 01_cover.jpg, 02_letter.docx,
03_budget.xlsx, 04_appendix.pdf works well. page2.pdf correctly comes before
page10.pdf.

To remove pages rather than pick them, put ! in front: report.pdf:!7 keeps
every page except page 7.

For exact control (specific pages, custom order, files from elsewhere),
rename order.txt.example to order.txt in the folder above and edit it.

Then double-click Merge.bat. Result appears in ..\output\merged.pdf

Prefer a window? Double-click "Merge GUI.bat" in the folder above instead, or
drop files straight onto it. It can do everything described here, plus a
contents page and page numbering, without touching this folder at all.

See README.md for the full set of options.
